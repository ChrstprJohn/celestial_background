# Brag Plan: Celestial

22 seconds, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps. A quiet product film for later website embedding, using the requested latent-spaces/brag workflow and Hyperframes renderer. Desktop, tablet, and phone frames contain captures of the current working website. The existing website is the visual authority.

## Nine-question planning rubric

1. Celestial lets visitors discover a personal date in NASA's sky, calculate a Moon phase, shuffle astronomy photographs, explore eight planets, and meet six cosmic pets.
2. Strongest copy: “Your place among the stars.”
3. Visual hook: real spiral-galaxy imagery in the midnight/lavender site, revealed inside a large laptop.
4. Show the actual homepage, Birthday sky result, Moon date/result, Saturn-to-Earth selection, and a cosmic pet on a phone.
5. 22 seconds gives the date interaction and device ensemble enough settled reading time.
6. Polished, with cinematic restraint: slow camera moves, crisp reveals, serif typography, no loud effects.
7. Original soft ambient music, supported by three restrained Brag UI accents. No narration.
8. Caption: “Celestial. Find the sky on a day that matters. A birthday sky, your Moon, eight worlds, and a few cosmic companions.”
9. Entry → choose a date or planet → see the actual image, Moon phase, or planet. Simulate the Moon date-picker selection and the Earth selection using authentic before/after captures. Date results now update automatically in the current site; captures were refreshed after those changes appeared during production.

## Identity

Night #070b17; lavender #c9c1f0; foreground #f3f0e9; secondary #b5bbd0. Instrument Serif regular/italic with DM Sans. Locally shipped fonts. Restrained stars inherit the product setting. Device hardware is dark silver; site content stays unchanged inside it.

## Audio direction

Original warm ambient pads and quiet bell notes, composed for the scene timing. Fade in 0.7 seconds, fade out 1.5 seconds. Sparse soft reveal and selection accents from Brag's Kenney library. No narration. Beat detection and audio-reactivity are intentionally unnecessary for this slow, non-rhythmic film. No claims of beat sync.

## Deliverables

Music MP4, muted embedding MP4, best-frame JPG poster baked into frame zero, editable Hyperframes composition, share copy, and a drop-in HTML embed example. Keep exports local; page insertion can happen later.
